SYNORA '26 GUIDELINES

📜 Terms & Conditions

1. Registration & Team Composition (Min 2, Max 4)
Participation is open to undergraduate and postgraduate students. Teams must consist of a minimum of 2 and maximum of 4 members (1 Team Leader + 1 mandatory Member, plus up to 2 optional Members). All submitted details must be authentic and verifiable with valid college ID proof.
⚠️ 2. Fee Policy & Strict Non-Refund Rule
Once registration fee (₹100 per person for external teams) is completed, no refund or repayment will be entertained under any circumstances, including cancellation, team changes, or non-attendance.
3. Academic Integrity & Original Work
All prototypes, software code, and hardware designs must be created during the official hackathon sprint timeline on August 28, 2026. Pre-built projects or plagiarism will result in immediate disqualification.
🪪 4. Mandatory College ID Cards & Venue Check-In
All students must bring their official college ID cards. Physical ID verification is mandatory for entry into SIMATS Engineering campus and desk check-in. All teams must report to the Department of Medical Biotechnology, NEW SCAD Innovation Hub by 08:00 AM IST on August 28, 2026 with their downloaded digital PDF pass and physical college ID cards.
5. Code of Conduct & Jury Decisions
Participants must adhere to professional decorum. The decisions of the judging committee and organizers regarding evaluation, prizes, and certificates are final and binding.
